# Research Methodology

## Three separate questions

- **Topic clustering:** what posts discuss. Lexical or semantic clusters help sampling and retrieval; cluster membership says nothing about sharing causes.
- **Propagation mechanism:** whether a defined textual or contextual feature associates with reach after accounting for author, audience, timing, topic and selection. Observational comparisons remain associational.
- **Real effect:** whether the user's independently written posts perform better on prespecified outcomes in repeated publication tests. X distribution is nonrandom; report uncertainty and alternative explanations.

## Collection and measurement

Register discovery query, channel, source URL, UTC observation time and inclusion decision. Sample across topic, author and time; seek ordinary posts through the same author/topic/window instead of selecting only viral search hits. Preserve missing metrics as UNKNOWN; never interpret missing as zero. Search index counts can be stale, language-translated or inconsistently ordered, so record only unambiguous values and their raw evidence. Distinguish views from unique people or engagement; X documents repeat views as multiple counts. Store short research excerpts, not wholesale copyrighted posts.

## Hypothesis protocol

1. State feature, population, comparator, primary outcome and expected direction before examining holdout data. Record author/date/topic and media/link context.
2. Build exploration set and independent holdout split by author and preferably later time. Freeze definitions and exclusions before holdout evaluation.
3. Match within author where feasible, then compare topic and similar age/window. Examine medians and distributions, not only extreme examples; include negative cases and missingness tables.
4. Run sensitivity checks excluding promoted/quoted posts, large accounts, cross-posted media and unclear metric snapshots. Report uncertainty and selection bias; do not call observational correlations causal.
5. A formula begins HYPOTHESIS. REPLICATED requires independent authors/time samples with the same prespecified direction and documented counterexamples. EXPERIMENT_SUPPORTED additionally requires repeated tests on the user's own independently written posts with actual outcome feedback. No automatic numeric threshold promotes states. REJECTED and REVIEW_REQUIRED preserve history.

When own posts are available, compare to the account's recent baseline and repeated similar topic/time slots. Record impressions, likes, replies, reposts, profile visits and follows separately. Avoid maximizing raw views at the expense of accuracy or audience quality. With no own-account data, there is no experimental support.

## Upworthy transfer limit

The [Upworthy Research Archive](https://upworthy.natematias.com/) separated exploratory headline experiments from confirmatory tests and randomized variants for the same story. Its [data paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-021-00934-7) describes 32,487 experiments with impressions and clicks. This supports preregistration, same-story controls and holdouts as methods. Its English-language click outcome on a publisher site does not transfer directly to Chinese X view/repost behavior.
