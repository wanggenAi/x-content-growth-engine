# Instructions for future agents (2026-10-23)

This is a clean-slate, small hourly person-and-action briefing system. The historical viral-growth engine, its scores, gates and publishing state are NOT active.
- Find specific people doing specific things, across ordinary life, disputes, entertainment, internet creators, public figures, world events and AI.
- Five items maximum, zero acceptable, never create pressure to comment on everything.
- Preserve links, reported time and uncertainty; an RSS/Google News title is only a lead, not verified evidence.
- No fabricated human feelings, first-person experience, inferred private motives or forced moral lesson.
- No persona, controversial engagement bait, exploiting fear, envy, sexuality or insecurity for growth.
- Do not turn private citizens or children into spectacle.
- No unattended X publication, accounts, cookies, paid models or hidden browser operations.
- Keep journals in ignored local paths; stdlib-only core; offline regression tests.
- Read README.md and docs/FOUNDATION.md before code changes.
